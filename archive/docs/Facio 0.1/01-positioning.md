# 01 — Positioning

## Problem

People do not lack information. They lack **execution structure**.

They fail because:

1. Intention is vague (“I want to get fit”).
2. The first mile is expensive (“what exactly tomorrow?”).
3. Life interrupts.
4. Tools fragment the job (chat, notes, calendar, habits) — none owns **completion**.

Facio owns the gap between **want** and **done**.

## Promise

Facio transforms any intention into an executable **Guide** and **leads** the user through daily **Sessions** until the result is reached.

It does **not** primarily:

- answer questions
- help “plan better”
- motivate with quotes
- host an AI conversation

If ChatGPT answers *“How do I…?”*,  
Facio answers *“What do I do right now?”* — after the user has trusted the path.

## Positioning lines

**Short:** AI guide for anything you want to do.

**Longer:** Facio transforms “I want…” into an executable guide and helps you complete it one session at a time.

**Slogan:** Guides you.

**Value sold:** movement / accompaniment of execution — not knowledge, not plan generation theater.

## Main product hypothesis

> Users lack structure of execution, not information.  
> Facio sells movement.

## North-star loop

```text
I want...
  → Clarify
  → Guide (trust / Explore)
  → Commitment
  → Daily Sessions (Execute)
  → Done
  → Repair (if life intervenes)
  → Next cycle / next Guide
```

## Why users return

Not for AI. Not for chat. Not for a pretty plan.

Because the app already prepared the **next executable Session**.

Facio shortens the path from *“I want”* to *“I did”*.

## Audience (primary)

Behavior, not category:

> People who constantly want to start something and finish it.

Examples: side-project builders, students, self-improvement enthusiasts, people with many small life goals.

## Go-to-market wedge

Do **not** launch as “universal AI”.

Enter through concrete scenarios that share one execution engine:

- cook a dish
- training program
- language learning
- ship a pet project
- home projects
- learn a skill

Prototype dogfood already used **carbonara** + **push-ups → ~30**. Keep those as quality bars while expanding scenarios carefully.

## Category claim

Not chat. Not productivity. Not habits.

**Guide / intention-to-execution system** — daily leading through Sessions with UI Blocks.

If we cannot defend that, we die as “ChatGPT + tasks”.

## Non-goals (product boundaries)

Facio is **not**:

- ChatGPT / general assistant
- Todoist / Notion / calendar
- Habit streak casino
- Fitness-only or recipes-only app
- Language tutor as the product (language can be *a* Guide)
- Medical device / therapist chatbot
- Content marketplace first

Those domains may live **inside** one execution UX — they are not separate products.

## Success signals (product-level)

Users say:

- “I finished the carbonara Guide.”
- “I just open Facio and do the Session.”
- “It rebuilt the week after I got sick.”

Not: “cool chat”, “nice plan”, “lots of AI messages”.

Prefer metrics of: time-to-first-Session, Session completion, Guide completion / repair recovery — over tokens and messages.

## Philosophy in one line

**Execution first. AI second.**

AI makes creating and repairing Guides faster. AI is not the home screen.
