# Risks

## Product risks

| Risk | Why lethal | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Universal positioning | mediocre at everything | wedge domains + quality bars |
| Becomes ChatGPT UI | no retention vs free chat | home = next action |
| Becomes todo/habit app | no differentiation | outcome contracts + evidence |
| Over-asking onboarding | drop before plan | 5-question budget |
| Plan theater | pretty plans, no action | activation metric |
| Notification hell | uninstall | preference learning, relevance |

## Technical risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| LLM nondeterminism breaks schedules | compiler + patch validation |
| Offline/sync bugs | CRDT-lite events, idempotency |
| Taxonomy entropy | governance, merges |
| Cost spikes | budgets, playbook-first, caches |
| Embedding model drift | versioned re-embed jobs |

## Safety / legal risks

| Domain | Risk | Mitigation |
|--------|------|------------|
| Health / weight | ED, injury | policy classifier, disclaimers, restrict |
| Medical advice | liability | refuse diagnose/treat; refer out |
| Finance | bad advice | educational framing, no personalized securities advice without licenses |
| Minors | heightened duty | age gates, limited domains |
| Data | privacy regs | minimization, retention, consent |

**Do not** hand-wave this because “AI said it.” You ship the app.

## Market risks

- AI feature fatigue
- platform fees / ATT notification permission decline
- incumbents adding “AI plans” checkboxes
- users want human coach, not software

## Organizational risks

- content production can’t keep up with marketing promises
- team builds model demos instead of execution engine
- no owner for blueprint quality

## Strategic risk: success metric confusion

Optimizing Coach engagement can increase revenue short-term while destroying the deterministic product long-term.

Guard with attainment and playbook resolution KPIs.

## Top risk (editorial opinion)

**Coverage ambition exceeds blueprint quality.**  
Everything else is recoverable; a reputation for unsafe or useless plans is not.
