# Monetization

## Principle

Charge for **ongoing outcome insurance** (adaptation, coaching depth, multi-project power), not for the oxygen of seeing today’s task.

Paywalling next actions after onboarding destroys trust and reviews.

## Value metrics users will pay for

1. “It keeps me going when I fall off”
2. “It rebuilds the plan when life happens”
3. “It gets me to a result faster / safer”
4. “It works across multiple serious projects”
5. (Later) premium content packs / expert blueprints

## Packaging (recommended v1)

### Free — “Start & Execute”

- limited number of active projects (e.g. 1)
- full deterministic execution for supported blueprints
- basic reminders
- playbook-based repairs (most)
- very limited Coach turns (e.g. 2/month) OR Coach only after stall with cap

### Facio Plus — “Repair & Accelerate”

- more active projects (e.g. 3)
- higher Coach caps
- advanced analytics / weekly review insights
- priority blueprint variants
- smarter rescheduling features

### (Later) Facio Pro / Teams

- career / org goals
- coach seat for human trainers using Facio as OS
- API / export

## What not to sell early

- token packs (feels metering of AI, not outcomes)
- gacha motivation
- social likes
- “AI girlfriend coach” skins

## Pricing intuition (not final)

Consumer sub in local market + optional annual.

Anchor to categories users already pay:

- fitness apps
- education apps
- habit apps premium

Not to ChatGPT Plus (wrong comparison set for mainstream).

## Expansion revenue

- paid expert blueprint packs (chef, physio-designed progressions) with clear liability framing
- B2B2C: gyms, clinics (careful), employers wellness — only after consumer retention proven

## Unit economics constraints

- Free onboarding must be cheap (template path)
- Plus gross margin target > 70% after LLM
- Cap abuse: Coach not unbounded chat

## Ethical monetization

- no hostage: export plan data
- no shame paywalls (“you failed — pay to continue”)
- sensitive domains: do not upsell aggressively during crisis language

## Critique of “Coach = subscription”

Good primary wedge **after** free execution proves value.

If users never experience completion on free, Coach upsell is lipstick on a todo list.

Sequence:

1. nail free completion loops in wedge domain
2. then monetize repair depth
