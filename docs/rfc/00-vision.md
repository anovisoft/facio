# 00 — Vision

## The problem we actually solve

Getting good advice is solved. An assistant will explain how to train, how to cook, how to structure a project — on demand, for free, at a quality that was not available five years ago.

What is not solved: **the answer has nowhere to live.** It arrives inside a conversation, it is correct, and it evaporates before the moment it was meant for.

The founding case, in full:

1. Decide to do something physical. Push-ups: 4 reps now, 30 in a row as the target.
2. Talk to an AI. Get a real progression.
3. Week two: the lower back starts taking the load.
4. Talk to the AI again. Real conclusion: brace the whole core and the glutes so the back does not carry it.
5. Now at 28 reps — **and that cue is forgotten on almost every set.**
6. Also forgotten: today’s number, the one the progression depends on.
7. The exercise bike: put off **three weeks**. Not refused — forgotten, then remembered at 21:40 with the nearest gym already shut.
8. “Eat vegetables more often”: never became anything at all.

Every piece of advice was correct. Nobody was lazy. The conclusions had nowhere to live, so they evaporated between the conversation and the doing.

Note the shape of it: **every step began with “I want this and I don’t know how.”** The assistant is where the method comes from — that is why the loop starts at all, and why it will keep starting. The failure is always downstream of a good answer.

Typical split:

- Chat produces the conclusion and forgets it.
- Notes store the cue but never open themselves.
- Habit apps hold the cadence but not the reason.
- Calendars hold time but not the doing.
- Specialist trackers log the work but not why it changed.

The previous Facio tried to own **completion of short goals**. Wrong center: a carbonara night ends, and a back that gave out in week two does not.

Facio’s job: **give the answer a body.** A conclusion becomes a mechanic that comes back on time, carrying its reason.

## North star

> A person asks how to do something, gets a real answer, and that answer comes back by itself — on the right day, at an hour that still works, with the reason attached — for months. Not a better plan. A plan that does not evaporate.

Two halves, and both are needed. **Materialising the answer once** is the entry: it is why the app is opened for a new thing. **Bringing it back with its reason** is the product: it is why the app is still open in week six. Ship only the first and we are an export button on somebody else’s chat.

## Product category

Not “AI chat.” Not “productivity.” Not “habits.” Not a smart daily planner. Not “Outcome OS.”

**Category claim:** the place where **advice becomes a practice that holds its own pace**.

Three things must live in one object, or we are somebody else already:

| Piece | Alone it is | Who owns it today |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Reason** — brace the core; the gym shuts at 22 | a note | ChatGPT, Notes |
| **Cadence** — three times a week | a streak | habit apps |
| **Drift** — untouched for three weeks | a guilt badge | nobody, honestly |

Ship any one of the three alone and we lose to whoever already owns it.

### Neighbors (say this out loud)

| Class | Examples | What they are |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Blank-prompt assistant | ChatGPT, Claude | Produce conclusions, keep none on a clock |
| Habit / streak app | Streaks, Habitica | Cadence with no reason and no memory of why it changed |
| Smart daily planner | Sunsama, Amie, Motion, Reclaim | Calendar + tasks; AI *schedules* the day |
| Specialist runtime | Hevy, Strong; cooking YouTube | Log or teach one vertical, deeper than we will be |
| Old Facio | archive 0.1 | Finite Guide → Session planner |

Planners: their object is *about* the work (“workout at 19:00”). Ours carries **why this workout changed**.

Specialists: deeper inside their vertical, and indifferent to why.

Habit apps are the closest and the most instructive: they got the cadence right and threw away the reason, which is why week three is a checkbox nobody remembers agreeing to.

### Why not Hevy + Notes + a habit app? (say this out loud)

Because one real week held push-ups, an exercise bike, vegetables, and a side project.

- Hevy has no vegetables and no project. It logs sets; it does not know the lower back gave out in week two.
- Notes hold “brace the core” perfectly and never open themselves at rep one.
- A habit app ticks the bike and cannot say *go at 19:00, the gym shuts at 22*.
- ChatGPT produced every one of those conclusions and dropped all of them.

Nobody carries **reason + cadence + drift** in one object. That is the entire gap.

Wedge line: **the advice you already got, coming back on time, with its reason.**  
Honesty line: **not specialist-grade inside any vertical — we carry the practice, not the deepest screen for it.**

If someone only lifts and never drifts, they should use Hevy. Facio is for a week that is mixed and for intentions that quietly go missing.

## Wedge

“Universal lifelong assistant” is a terrible launch wedge. So is “two deep verticals.”

**Breadth of subjects is cheap. Depth inside one runtime is expensive.** Push-ups are a counter with a cue. Vegetables are a tick with a cadence. The bike is a reminder with a window. None of the three competes with a specialist, and all three test the thesis.

| Phase | What we prove | Why |
|-------|----------------|-----|
| 0 | **One subject keeps its reason** — push-ups: today’s number, the core cue at do-time, a check-in that updates both | The conclusion survived the conversation |
| 1 | **Several subjects at once** — bike, vegetables, a project step | The desk; attention competing honestly |
| 2 | **Drift becomes visible** — the three-week bike says so itself, and offers to shrink | The half nobody solves |
| 3 | Depth in one runtime (cook timeline, richer stepper), voice, richer memory | Only after persistence works |

**Decision rule:** add a *subject* whenever the user has one — that is free. Do **not** add a *deep runtime* until reason + cadence + drift hold across several shallow subjects.

Open risk worth naming: the three physical subjects are short and verifiable. A project step is open-ended, and “I make plans and abandon them” is about will, not memory. Assume the mechanic holds for the first kind; treat the second as unproven ([07](./07-open-questions.md) Q29).

## Success definition

Facio wins when people say:

- “It reminded me about my core right as I went down.”
- “It told me at 19:00, not at 21:40 with the gym already shut.”
- “I’d been dodging the bike two weeks and it said so — and offered to make it once a week.”
- “It knows I’m at 28 and what today’s number is.”

Not: “cool chat,” “nice plan,” “lots of AI,” “it planned my calendar,” “look at my streak.”

### Vision-level metrics

| Metric | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Cue hit rate at do-time** | The founding pain: was the reason there when the hands were? |
| **Drift surfaced within one cadence period** | The bike sat three weeks. That is the number to beat |
| **Subjects still alive at week 4** | Persistence is the actual claim |
| **Conclusion → mechanic rate** | Share of talks ending in something with a cadence, not only text |
| D1 open to lid | Home is the lid feed, not a prompt |
| Widget execution | Counters / timers / checklists actually used |
| Morning check-in completion | Rhythm, not pep talk |
| Cost per active day | Survival; **no number in this pack** — measure in implementation |

Vanity to deprioritize: messages sent, tokens, plans generated, widgets on screen, facts stored but never surfaced, streak length.

“It knows me” is a weeks-long hit-rate effect, not a day-0 promise.

## Non-goals (vision boundaries)

Facio is **not** trying to be:

- a general assistant for email / the whole internet
- ChatGPT with a wallpaper
- a Hevy / Strong / cooking-YouTube clone
- Sunsama / Motion: auto-scheduling a workday
- a streak casino
- a widget construction kit the user must learn
- a social network
- a medical device, a coach, or a therapist
- a content marketplace or Intent Economy in v1

## Vision test

For any feature:

1. Does it help a **conclusion survive** to the moment of doing?
2. Does it carry the **reason**, or only the task?
3. Can the daily loop run **without** an LLM call?
4. Would this still matter if LLMs were free and perfect tomorrow?
5. If it is only a checkbox on a schedule, would a habit app already do it?
6. Are we claiming depth inside a vertical a specialist already owns?

If (1) is no, reject. If (2) is “only the task,” we shipped a habit app. If (6) is our claim, we are lying.
