# 00 — Vision

## The problem we actually solve

People do not fail because information is scarce. They fail because there is no **place that stays in rhythm** with their day — and no counterpart that can work that place with them.

Typical split:

- Chat answers “how?” and forgets.
- Notes and todos store lists that do not run.
- Calendars store time but not the doing.
- Habit apps store streaks, not the work.

The previous Facio tried to own **completion of short goals**. That is still useful. It is the wrong **center**. A carbonara night ends. Life does not. A product that only exists as a planner for finite quests has no honest daily reason to open after the quest is done.

Facio’s job is to own the **desk of today**, for years — and still be able to run a recipe or a training session on that same desk.

## North star

> A person opens Facio, sees what matters today as live widgets, speaks to an assistant to change or understand that desk, and comes back tomorrow because the desk already knows the rhythm — not because they need another generated plan.

## Product category

Not “AI chat.” Not “productivity.” Not “habits.” Not “Outcome OS.” Not a **smart daily planner**.

**Category claim:** personal desk of **executable** widgets + assistant.

If we cannot defend the desk, we die as “ChatGPT with extra steps.”  
If the desk is only checklists, reminders, and calendar blocks, we die as a weaker Sunsama.  
If we claim the stepper is a better Hevy, we die on first dogfood with a lifter.

### Neighbors (say this out loud)

| Class | Examples | What they are |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Blank-prompt assistant | ChatGPT, Claude | Talk, no live desk |
| Smart daily planner | Sunsama, Amie, Motion, Reclaim | Calendar + tasks; AI *schedules* the day |
| **Specialist runtime** | Hevy, Strong; YouTube / NYT Cooking on the stove | One vertical, years on one screen |
| Old Facio | archive 0.1 | Finite Guide → Session planner |

Planners: their task is *about* the work (“workout at 19:00”). Ours **is** the work (stepper, cook timeline, timer).

Specialists: they **are** the work, deeper than we will be in that vertical. We do not win by merging ten shallow Hevys under a chat.

### Why not Hevy + Notes? (say this out loud)

Hevy is better at a lifting session. A video against the backsplash is better at teaching a knife cut. Notes never forget a shopping list that you actually open.

People fail **between** those apps: the cook is not in Hevy, Hevy does not know last time was undersalted, the timer is a different app, ChatGPT forgot, Notes does not run. Facio is the **desk that holds the mixed week** and can talk to it.

If someone only lifts, they should use Hevy. Facio is for a week that is mixed and for follow-through across subjects — not for replacing a specialist’s one screen.

Wedge line: **today’s desk, where you cook and train with your hands — not only check off “do it.”**  
Honesty line: **good enough to do tonight without leaving the lid; not specialist-grade in every vertical.**

Do not add a third executable vertical until carbonara **or** a training day is good enough that a casual user does not immediately prop YouTube / download Hevy *for that evening*. Two bars, not ten apps.

Read that bar narrowly, or it contradicts the honesty line. It is about a **casual** user **mid-run**: they already chose this dish or this session, and they get through it on the lid without opening a second app in the middle. It is not “deeper than Hevy,” and it is not an enthusiast comparing feature lists — that person is out of scope by the table above. Specialist-grade is not the bar. **Finishing tonight without a second screen** is.

Also keep: no morning “plan your day for 10 minutes” (morning is a check); memory is life (salt, not before 19:00), not a workgraph.

## Wedge

“Universal lifelong assistant” is a terrible launch wedge. It maximizes coverage debt and marketing fuzz.

| Phase | What we prove | Why |
|-------|----------------|-----|
| 0 | **Today’s desk** — tiles + composer + morning check | Daily reason to open; tests the metaphor |
| 1 | **Executable widgets** — checklist, timer, counter, stepper, timeline | The old Facio strength; the anti-planner wedge |
| 2 | **Cook / train on the same desk** — carbonara, a training day | Proof that plans are projections, not a second product |
| 3 | Voice, richer memory, more types | After the desk works in silence |

**Decision rule:** do not add a widget type, a proactive channel, or a domain until the current desk is used without explanation.

Dogfood bars from the old engine still apply: carbonara-class cook and push-ups-class training must feel like *doing*, not like a ChatGPT recipe pasted into todos.

## Success definition

Facio wins when people say:

- “I just open it and see today.”
- “I said add garlic — it was in the recipe.”
- “I asked what brisket is, then cooked.”
- “In the morning it asked about carbonara, I said it was undersalted, next time it remembered.”

Not: “cool chat,” “nice plan,” “lots of AI,” “it planned my calendar.”

### Vision-level metrics

| Metric | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **D1 open to lid** | Home is the lid feed (Today), not the pan or a prompt |
| **Widget execution** | Timers / steppers / checklists actually used |
| **Desk mutation via assistant** | Talk can change widgets |
| **Grounded Q&A** | Explain-the-widget turns are allowed; they are not the north star |
| **Memory hit rate** | Share of new placements where a fact was applied *and shown* |
| **Morning check-in completion** | Rhythm, not pep talk |
| **Return when Today has work** | Lifelong — not fake daily padding |
| **Subject repeat (e.g. weekly cook)** | Memory hit rate actually pays off |
| **Cost per active day** | Survival; **no number in this pack** — measure in implementation |

Vanity to deprioritize: messages sent, tokens, plans generated, widgets on screen, facts stored but never used.

“It knows me” is a weeks-long hit-rate effect, not a day-0 promise.

## Non-goals (vision boundaries)

Facio is **not** trying to be:

- a general assistant for email / the whole internet
- ChatGPT with a wallpaper
- a Hevy / Strong / cooking-YouTube clone
- Sunsama / Motion: auto-scheduling a workday
- a widget construction kit the user must learn
- a social network
- a medical device or therapist
- a content marketplace or Intent Economy in v1

## Vision test

For any feature:

1. Does it make the **desk** clearer or more executable?
2. Does it reduce time from “I want” to **something on the desk**?
3. Can the daily loop run **without** an LLM call?
4. Would this still matter if LLMs were free and perfect tomorrow?
5. If it is only checklist + calendar, would a daily planner already do it?  
6. If they only do this one sport/craft, would the specialist already do it better? If yes, we are not trying to steal that person *this year*.

If (4) is only “more chat,” reject. If (5) is yes, it is not the wedge. If (6) is our launch claim, we are lying.
