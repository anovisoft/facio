# Product Philosophy

## Thesis

**Users do not buy AI. They buy progress they can feel.**

AI is the factory floor. The shelf product is:

- clarity of next action
- a plan that survives contact with reality
- proof that something is changing
- recovery when they fall off

## From conversation to commitment

Most AI products optimize for:

- fluency
- engagement
- message volume

Facio optimizes for:

- **commitment** (structured project created)
- **action** (next step done)
- **continuity** (project doesn’t die after day 3)
- **completion** (outcome criteria met)

This implies UX that is often *less chatty*, not more.

## Micro-projects, not chats

Every accepted intention becomes a **Project** with:

- outcome definition
- constraint profile
- plan graph
- schedule
- evidence model
- status (active / paused / stalled / completed / abandoned)

The chat surface, if present, is subordinate: a *repair interface*, not the home screen.

### Critique of “ChatGPT but for goals”

If the home experience is a blank prompt, Facio will inherit ChatGPT’s failure mode: infinite planning, zero doing.

**Home screen should answer:** “What am I doing next, and why does it matter for my outcome?”

## Determinism by default

Philosophy for cost and trust:

1. Prefer **deterministic rules** over generative text.
2. Prefer **constrained generation** over freeform generation.
3. Prefer **retrieval of proven structures** over inventing new ones.
4. Prefer **local adaptation** over full replan.
5. Escalate to LLM Coach only when rules cannot resolve the situation.

This is not anti-AI. It is anti-waste and anti-hallucination.

## Outcome contracts

A plan without a success contract is entertainment.

Every project must define:

- **Success criteria** (binary or scored)
- **Evidence** (how we know progress happened)
- **Horizon** (expected duration / checkpoints)
- **Abort / pivot conditions**

Example — pull-ups:

- Success: 1 unassisted dead-hang pull-up
- Evidence: logged set with video optional / self-report with confidence
- Horizon: 8–12 weeks typical for beginner profile
- Pivot: shoulder pain → medical-safe path / deload blueprint

## Empathy without dependency

Good coaching reduces dependency over time.

Bad AI products create:

- endless reassurance loops
- “just ask the coach” as avoidance of action
- parasocial attachment

Facio’s coach should push toward **self-efficacy**: fewer interventions as the user stabilizes.

## Taste of the product

Facio should feel like:

- a calm project manager who already did the thinking
- a trainer with a clipboard, not a motivational Instagram

Not:

- a dopamine habit streak casino
- a therapist substitute
- a content feed

## Philosophical trade-offs we accept

| We choose | We sacrifice |
|-----------|--------------|
| Narrow excellence first | Universal “any goal” marketing early |
| Structured plans | Infinite creative freedom |
| Outcome evidence | Pure vibes / motivation quotes |
| Cost discipline | Always-on generative companionship |
| Repair loops | Perfect first plans |

## Product philosophy checklist

Before shipping a surface:

- [ ] Can a user act in < 60 seconds?
- [ ] Is the next action obvious without reading a paragraph?
- [ ] Can this screen work offline / without an LLM call?
- [ ] Does failure have a recovery path?
- [ ] Are we measuring completion, not engagement?
